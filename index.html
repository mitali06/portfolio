RiskIQ
Production Application

The Problem: Loan officers and risk analysts manually evaluate applications using inconsistent criteria, resulting in longer approval times, subjective decision-making, and difficulty scaling operations. Each assessment requires significant time and expertise to analyze debt ratios, creditworthiness, and financial stability.

The Solution: RiskIQ is an AI-powered financial risk assessment platform that automates the analysis of loan applications. In seconds, it evaluates an applicant's financial profile and generates quantified risk scores (0-100%), key financial metrics (debt-to-income, loan-to-income ratios), identifies risk factors and concerns, highlights financial strengths, and provides specific recommendations for approval or mitigation.

The Impact: By automating routine analysis, RiskIQ reduces assessment time from hours to seconds, provides consistent evaluation across all applications, eliminates subjective bias, and enables loan officers to focus on complex cases and client relationships rather than data compilation.
